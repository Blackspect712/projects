Services

From a single driveway to a full subdivision access plan — every project gets the same two-pass quality check.

01 — Roads

Road layout design

We design the geometry that ties everything together: alignment, carriageway widths, gradients, sight distances, and intersections. Every layout is checked against your local authority's standards before a single line reaches your drawing set.

  • Horizontal and vertical alignment
  • Width, camber, and gradient compliance
  • Intersection and sight-distance checks
  • Public and private access roads

Typical deliverables

  • Setting-out drawing with coordinates
  • Longitudinal sections and levels
  • Construction notes and legend sheet
  • Standards compliance summary

What we balance

  • House position and setbacks
  • Driveway sight lines and parking
  • Drainage paths and ground levels
  • Services access and easements
02 — Sites

Residential site plans

A good site plan makes a plot feel bigger than it is. We position the house, drive, and outdoor spaces so the whole property works — for the people living there and for the reviewer signing it off.

  • House placement and setback drawings
  • Driveway and parking layouts
  • Levels and drainage concept
  • Garden, boundary, and fencing plans
03 — Approvals

Approval drawings

Most plans don't fail on design — they fail on paperwork. We prepare complete, council-ready drawing sets with the notes, legends, and details reviewers expect, and we respond to comments so you don't have to decode them.

  • Full submission drawing sets
  • Reviewer comment responses
  • Revision tracking and reissues
  • Coordination with your surveyor or engineer

Submission checklist

  • Site plan to scale, north arrow, legend
  • Dimensions and setback annotations
  • Levels, gradients, and drainage notes
  • Standards references for your jurisdiction
04 — More

Feasibility, subdivision & review

Not every project needs a full drawing set on day one. Sometimes you need a straight answer first.

Feasibility studies

A fast, fixed-price assessment of whether a parcel can carry your idea — access, setbacks, and hard constraints included.

  • Access and frontage check
  • Buildable-area estimate
  • Red-flag constraint summary

Subdivision planning

Splitting one parcel into several — lot dimensions, corner sight triangles, shared access, and cul-de-sac design.

  • Lot layout and dimensioning
  • Easement and shared-driveway design
  • Subdivision submission support

Consultation & review

Already have plans? We'll check them against standards and give you a clear list of what to fix before submitting.

  • Second-opinion plan review
  • Written findings and priorities
  • Optional reissue of corrected drawings
