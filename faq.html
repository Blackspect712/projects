Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on timelines, pricing, and what happens after you get in touch.

How long does a typical project take?

Most residential road layouts and site plans are delivered within two weeks of the site visit. Full submission sets with reviewer comment rounds usually take three to five weeks depending on how fast comments come back.

How do you price your work?

Every project is quoted as a fixed price up front, based on the site size and the drawing set you need. You'll never receive an hourly invoice or a surprise addition at the end.

Do I need a survey before we start?

Not always. For a concept layout we can work from satellite imagery and public records. For submission drawings we'll need accurate survey data — if you don't have one yet, we can coordinate with a local surveyor.

Will you deal with the council on my behalf?

Yes. We prepare the submission documents, respond to reviewer comments, and reissue revised drawings. You'll always be copied in, but you won't have to decode the correspondence yourself.

What if my plan gets rejected?

With a 98% first-pass approval rate it's rare, but if it happens we address the reviewer's comments and reissue the drawings at no extra cost within the original scope of work.

Can you work with my architect or engineer?

Absolutely. We regularly coordinate with surveyors, civil engineers, and architects so that levels, structures, and services all line up in one consistent set of drawings.

Do you work outside the local area?

Yes — the studio has worked with more than 40 councils. Site visits are local; everything else can be handled remotely by email, phone, and video call.

Still have a question?

Send it over — we answer every enquiry within two business days.
