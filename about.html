About the studio

One planner, twelve years of drawings, and a stubborn habit of checking everything twice.

Our story

Started with a single subdivision

Meridian Planning began when its founder spent three months untangling a stalled subdivision — not because the design was wrong, but because the drawings never matched the standards. That project set the rule the studio still follows today: if it isn't clear enough for a reviewer to approve without a phone call, it isn't finished.

Since then the studio has put more than 120 plans through councils across the region, from single-family driveways to shared access roads serving a dozen lots.
